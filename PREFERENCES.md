# PREFERENCES — Laurel's Video Taste

Accumulated taste for video work in this workspace. **Read before every video task**; these rules override skill and `MOTION_PHILOSOPHY.md` defaults. Rebuilt 2026-11-18 from feedback given across earlier sessions.

---

## Visual style

- Light, calm, editorial — not the dark/chrome/neon MOTION_PHILOSOPHY look. (2026-10)
- Simple static text that stays on screen the whole time performs best on Reels. (2026-10-29)
- Text must never cover Laurel's face; reposition the photo or the text. (2026-11)
- Use photos where her eyes are open — no squinting in bright sun. CTA slides need friendly eye contact / a smile. (2026-11-11, 2026-11-18)
- More space at the top of reels: top text at ~210px. (2026-11-11)
- Never stretch footage; crop 4:3 clips to fill. (2026-10-29)
- Carousel text must never be clipped — give stacked text boxes enough line-height. (2026-11-06)

## Pacing

- Reels 10–15s, text static; Stories 10–15s per slide. (2026-10)

## Captions

- Every Reel/carousel needs a caption that delivers the promise; client stories make good captions. (2026-11)
- Questions/emoji prompts ("How do you feel when you wake up? Drop an emoji") beat "save this" for engagement. (2026-10-29)

## Transitions

- Calm only (blur/soft); no glitch or whip. (2026-10)

## Audio

- No music baked in — added in Instagram with a trending sound. (2026-10)

## Brand

- Navy #001f3f / #ebe6e5 boxes / soft blue #94b4da / terracotta #c76d62. (2026-10)
- CTAs drive to the Hormone Assessment via link in bio. (2026-11)

## Avoid

- "See your doctor" CTAs — Laurel can help. (2026-10-22)
- "Comment X" on every reel — rotate endings. (2026-10-29)
- Weak CTAs like "Save this and come back to it tonight". (2026-10-29)
- Ad-looking Stories — keep Stories personal ("I had a client this week…"). (2026-11-04)
- Reusing a clip on the same topic. (2026-10-29)
- Myths without context — the myth slide must make sense on its own. (2026-11-11)
- Alcohol in hormone-content footage. (2026-11-16)
