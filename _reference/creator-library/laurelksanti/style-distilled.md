# @laurelksanti — Style Fingerprint (baseline: her own posts)

Rolled up from 7 past posts studied 2026-10-19, plus results since. Use it to keep voice and topics consistent; the **Level-up plan** is the default direction.

## Baseline (before)
- Format: one silent clip of Laurel (5–9s) with one static text overlay; no cuts, all text on screen from frame 0.
- Hooks: strong — contrarian ("Your lunch salad can bloat you more than pizza…"), curiosity ("What do estrogen dominance, constipation… have in common?"), identity ("Behind every woman who…").
- Weaknesses: walls of text, inconsistent caption styles, text over her face, hooks not paid off, no CTA.

## What works now
- **Simple checklist text reels** ("Signs your estrogen may be running too high") = best performer.
- Problem-first hooks, 3–6 short lines, a rotating ending (save / share / statement / occasional keyword).
- Carousels in the navy-on-#ebe6e5 box style; personal Stories in Instagram-text-tool style.

## Level-up plan
1. One idea per line; listicles 12–15s so every point is readable.
2. One consistent branded text system per format (see CREATOR.md → Brand identity).
3. Text never over her face; top text starts ~210px.
4. Always pay off the hook on screen or in the caption.
5. Try talking Reels with her recorded clips (familiarity is the content gap).

## Don't
- Walls of static text, mixed fonts post-to-post, unanswered hooks.
- Health claims as fact without nuance.
